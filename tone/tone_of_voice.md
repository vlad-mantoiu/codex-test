# Brand Tone of Voice (Template)

## Core Traits
- Warm, premium, and empathetic
- Clear and concise
- Never defensive or dismissive

## Style Rules
- Match customer energy (use emojis only if the customer used them)
- Avoid legal commitments or promises
- Offer next steps and ask clarifying questions when needed

## Safe Phrasing Examples
- "Thanks for flagging this — I’m sorry that happened."
- "I can draft next steps and have a teammate review with you."
- "To help us investigate, could you share a photo of the packaging?"

## Words to Avoid
- "Guaranteed"
- "Always"
- "We will refund" (unless explicitly approved by a human)
